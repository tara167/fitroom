01 The studio

Built for this.
Built for you.

Fit Room sits just off the beach in Playa Hermosa, Santa Teresa — bright, open, unhurried. It's a small space on purpose: five people, max, so every class feels personal instead of packed.

The studio is built on real Pilates standards — certified instructors, small classes, real progress — led with 30 years of teaching experience behind every detail, from how the space feels to how classes are taught.

This isn't a gym. It's an hour that's yours.

Founded by Eileen Ramírez · Santa Teresa

03 How to Book

Get on the reformer.

  1. 01

    Tap the link to open Fit Room Santa Teresa's booking page.

  2. 02

    Create your free account — just your name, email, and phone.

  3. 03

    Sign the studio's terms and take two quick photos (a face photo and, optionally, a profile photo). This keeps our studio secure and is a one-time step.

  4. 04

    Choose your class or package and pay by card, SINPE, or bank transfer.

  5. 05

    Pick your class time and you're booked. See you on the reformer!
